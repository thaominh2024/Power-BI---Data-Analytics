# Power BI Data Analytics Portfolio

A collection of Power BI projects focused on data visualization, business performance analysis, and interactive dashboard development.

These projects demonstrate practical skills in data exploration, KPI monitoring, DAX calculations, and transforming business data into understandable visualizations.

## Projects

### 1. Business Performance & Receivables Dashboard

An interactive Power BI report for monitoring business performance and outstanding receivables.

**Key Features**
- Revenue and commission analysis
- Actual vs. target performance monitoring
- Outstanding and overdue receivables tracking
- Branch-level and monthly comparisons
- Interactive slicers and visualizations

**Tools:** Power BI, DAX

**Project Folder:** `Business-Performance-Analysis/`

### 2. Sales Analytics & Time Intelligence

A Power BI project focused on analyzing sales quantity, profitability, and business performance across time periods.

**Key Features**
- Month-to-Date (MTD) and Year-to-Date (YTD) calculations
- Month-over-Month (MoM) comparisons
- Product and sales channel analysis
- Interactive charts and KPI reporting

**Tools:** Power BI, DAX

**Project Folder:** `Sales-Analytics-DAX/`

## Technical Skills Demonstrated

- Data Visualization
- Business Intelligence
- DAX Measures
- Time Intelligence
- KPI Tracking
- Interactive Dashboard Design
- Data Analysis

## How to View

1. Open the desired project folder.
2. Preview the dashboard screenshots.
3. Download the `.pbix` file.
4. Open it using Microsoft Power BI Desktop.
